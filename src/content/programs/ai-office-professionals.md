## Program Overview
The **AI for Office Professionals** course is built for people who work in offices and want to do their job in a fraction of the time using AI tools. This is not a technical program — no coding required. You will learn to use AI to write faster, analyze data automatically, design presentations in minutes, and automate repetitive tasks that used to take hours.

---

## Week 1–2: Excel + AI
- AI Formula Generation: Describe what you want, get the formula instantly
- **Copilot in Excel** — Natural language data analysis and chart generation
- **ChatGPT for Excel** — Debugging formulas, writing macros with plain English
- Data Cleaning Automation: Remove duplicates, standardize formats, fill missing values
- AI-Generated Dashboards: Visualize KPIs with minimal manual effort

---

## Week 3–4: Word + AI
- **AI Writing Assistant** — ChatGPT and Copilot for drafting reports, memos, and proposals
- **Professional Email Writing** — Tone matching, formal vs casual, reply generation
- AI-Powered Formatting: Auto-structure reports and apply templates
- Document Summarization: Condense long documents into key bullet points
- Minutes of Meeting: AI dictation and auto-formatting of meeting notes

---

## Week 5–6: PowerPoint + AI
- **Copilot in PowerPoint** — Generate entire presentations from a single prompt
- **Gamma AI** — Build polished decks from rough notes in under 2 minutes
- **Canva AI** — Drag-and-drop presentations with AI-generated visuals
- Slide summarization and redesign using AI suggestions

---

## Week 7–8: Email Automation + Office Workflows
- **AI Email Management** — Smart reply suggestions, triage, and follow-up automation
- **Meeting Notes with AI** — Otter.ai and Copilot for auto-transcription and action item extraction
- **Workflow Automation Basics** — Using Zapier and Make to connect Office apps automatically
- Build your first automation: Email → Spreadsheet → Notification pipeline
- Report Writing at Scale: Use AI templates and bulk generation workflows

---

## Practical Deliverables
1. Automated Excel dashboard with AI-generated analysis
2. AI-drafted monthly business report (Word + ChatGPT)
3. Professional presentation deck (Gamma AI or Copilot)
4. One Zapier/Make automation workflow that saves time daily
