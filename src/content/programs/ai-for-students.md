## Program Overview
The **AI Essentials for Students** is a 45-day crash course designed specifically for students. You will learn how to use AI tools to study smarter, research deeper, write better assignments, create impressive presentations, and prepare for exams — all while using AI responsibly and ethically.

---

## Week 1–2: Studying with AI
- **ChatGPT as a Study Companion** — Explain complex topics in simple language
- **NotebookLM** — Upload your textbooks and notes; ask questions, get summaries and quizzes
- **AI Flashcard Generation** — Create smart study cards from any document
- **Concept Mapping with AI** — Napkin AI and ChatGPT for visual mind maps
- **Exam Preparation** — Practice tests, mock Q&As, and topic prioritization with AI

---

## Week 3–4: AI Research Skills
- **Perplexity AI** — Cited, real-time research with source links (no hallucinations)
- **Consensus** — Find peer-reviewed academic papers on any topic instantly
- **NotebookLM for Literature Review** — Synthesize multiple research sources into a summary
- Citation Management — Proper academic referencing with AI assistance
- Fact-checking AI outputs — How to verify and cross-reference AI-generated information
- Avoiding plagiarism while using AI: Ethical best practices

---

## Week 5–6: AI-Powered Assignments & Presentations
- **AI Writing Assistant** — Drafting, structuring, and editing assignments with ChatGPT + Grammarly
- **Gamma AI** — Create professional presentations for class projects in minutes
- **Canva AI** — Design posters, infographics, and visual reports for assignments
- **AI for Video Summaries** — YouTube transcript summarization and lecture note generation
- **Voice-to-Text & Transcription** — Converting interviews and recordings into notes automatically

---

## Final Projects
1. **AI Research Report** — 3-page academic report written using Perplexity + NotebookLM + ChatGPT
2. **Class Presentation** — 8-slide AI-generated presentation using Gamma AI on a subject of choice
3. **Personal Study System** — A custom NotebookLM notebook with your subject notes, quizzes, and summaries
