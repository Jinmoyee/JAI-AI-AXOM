## Program Overview
The **PGDCA + AI** is JAI AI AXOM's most comprehensive program — a 12-month postgraduate curriculum designed for graduates who want to go beyond basic computer applications and master the full stack of modern AI systems. Split into two powerful semesters, this program takes you from advanced Office workflows to building real AI agents, apps, and data intelligence products.

---

## Semester 1

### Advanced Office + AI Integration
- **MS Word + AI** — Advanced formatting, mail merge, and Copilot document drafting
- **Excel + AI** — Power Query, complex formulas, AI formula generation, and AI dashboards
- **PowerPoint + AI** — Professional presentation design with AI-assisted layouts
- **Google Workspace AI** — Gemini integration across Docs, Sheets, and Gmail
- **AI Office Tools** — Microsoft Copilot enterprise workflows and team collaboration

### AI Fundamentals
Deep technical understanding of how modern AI systems work.

- **LLMs** — How large language models are trained, fine-tuned, and deployed
- **Memory Systems** — Short-term, long-term, and vector memory in AI applications
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** — Connecting LLMs to private knowledge bases
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** — Standard for connecting AI agents to external tools and data
- **AI Agents** — Building autonomous agents that plan, reason, and take actions
- **Local LLMs** — Running Ollama, LM Studio, and open-source models on local hardware

### Advanced Prompt Engineering
- Structured system prompts and instruction hierarchies
- Prompt chaining and multi-step reasoning
- Tool-use prompting and function calling
- Evaluating and benchmarking prompt quality
