## Program Overview
The **AI Professional Certificate** is a high-intensity 3-month program for anyone who wants to move fast. It skips the basics and goes straight into the core skills that AI professionals need: advanced prompting, generative AI tools, research workflows, automation pipelines, and building basic AI agents.

---

## Month 1 – Generative AI & Prompt Engineering

### Generative AI Essentials
- How LLMs, Diffusion Models, and Multimodal AI work
- Understanding context windows, temperature, and token limits
- Text, image, audio, and video generation — practical applications
- AI ethics, hallucination detection, and critical evaluation

### Advanced Prompt Engineering
- The anatomy of a great prompt (role, context, instruction, format, tone)
- Prompt frameworks: RACE, CRISPE, Tree-of-Thought
- Chain-of-Thought prompting for complex reasoning tasks
- Few-shot and zero-shot prompting strategies
- System prompts and building Custom GPT assistants
- Evaluating and iterating on prompt quality

---

## Month 2 – AI Research & Workflow Automation

### AI Research Tools
- **NotebookLM** — Build a personal AI knowledge assistant from your own documents
- **Perplexity AI** — Real-time, source-cited AI search for professional research
- **Consensus** — AI-powered academic research and evidence extraction
- **Elicit** — Literature review automation and research synthesis

### Workflow Automation
- **Make (Integromat)** — Visual no-code automation: scenarios, modules, and data mapping
- **Zapier** — Business automation and app connection workflows
- **n8n** — Advanced automation with AI nodes and conditional logic
- Real-world automations: lead capture, social scheduling, email sequences, CRM updates

---

## Month 3 – AI Agents & Real Projects

### AI Agent Building
- What are AI agents? Planning, reasoning, memory, and action loops
- Building simple agents using n8n and Make with AI nodes
- Voice agents and AI assistants with conversational memory
- Intro to MCP (Model Context Protocol) for tool integration

### Real Projects (Portfolio)
1. **AI-Powered Research Report** — Published using NotebookLM + Perplexity + Claude
2. **Business Automation Pipeline** — Lead-to-CRM workflow built in n8n or Make
3. **Custom AI Assistant** — Trained on a specific knowledge domain using ChatGPT Custom GPTs
4. **AI Agent Demo** — A working autonomous agent that performs a multi-step task
