## Program Overview
The **AI for Content Creators & Marketers** course teaches you how to build a professional content operation using AI. Instead of spending 8 hours creating one video or post, you'll learn to produce high-quality, high-volume content in a fraction of the time — using AI tools for every part of the workflow: writing, design, voice, video, and distribution.

---

## Week 1: AI Writing & Copywriting
- **ChatGPT for Copywriting** — Social media captions, YouTube scripts, blog posts, and ad copy
- **Content Calendars** — Generate a full month of content ideas with a single prompt
- **Hook Writing** — AI-generated opening lines that stop the scroll
- **SEO Blog Writing** — AI research, keyword integration, and meta descriptions
- **Email Newsletter** — Weekly AI-assisted newsletters in 10 minutes
- **Brand Voice Training** — Teaching ChatGPT to write in your specific style

---

## Week 2: AI Image & Design Creation
- **Midjourney** — Text-to-image generation for commercial-grade visuals
- **DALL-E 3 via ChatGPT** — Concept art, product mockups, and social graphics
- **Canva AI** — Magic Design, Magic Write, Background Remover, and AI image expansion
- **Thumbnail Design** — YouTube thumbnail best practices with AI-assisted layout
- **Brand Kit Creation** — Consistent colors, fonts, and styles using Canva AI
- **Prompt Strategies for Images** — Lighting, style, mood, and composition prompting

---

## Week 3: AI Video & Reels Production
- **CapCut AI** — Auto-captions, video enhancement, background removal, and script-to-video
- **Runway Gen-2** — Text-to-video, image-to-video, and style transfer
- **Sora / Kling / Luma** — AI video generation from prompts (latest tools)
- **YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels** — AI-optimized short-form video workflow
- **Auto-Subtitles & Translations** — Reach international audiences with AI captioning
- **B-Roll & Stock AI** — AI-enhanced stock video selection and repurposing

---

## Week 4: AI Voice, SEO & Monetization
- **ElevenLabs** — Clone your own voice or create custom AI narration voices
- **Text-to-Speech for Videos** — Professional-quality AI voiceovers for YouTube and podcasts
- **YouTube SEO with AI** — Title, description, and tag optimization using VidIQ + ChatGPT
- **Content Repurposing Pipeline** — Turn one video into 10+ pieces of content using AI
- **Monetization Strategy** — AdSense, brand deals, digital products, and AI content services
- **Freelancing as an AI Creator** — Sell AI content creation services on Fiverr and Upwork

---

1. **AI Content Campaign** — 7-day social media campaign (captions + images) for a real or mock brand

3. **AI Reel** — 30-second Instagram Reel created using CapCut AI and Runway
