## Program Overview
The **Diploma in Computer Applications + AI (DCA-AI)** is Assam Cognexa Institute's flagship 6-month program that transforms complete beginners into confident, job-ready professionals. Unlike traditional DCA programs, every module in this course is taught alongside its AI-powered equivalent — so you learn to work smarter, faster, and more creatively from day one.

---

## Module 1 – Computer Fundamentals
Build a solid foundation before moving into AI tools.

- **Windows** – Navigation, settings, file systems, and shortcuts
- **Internet** – Browsing, search techniques, downloads, and online safety
- **Email** – Gmail & Outlook: composing, organizing, and professional email writing
- **File Management** – Organizing documents, folders, and cloud storage
- **Google Workspace** – Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, and Meet
- **Cyber Security** – Passwords, phishing awareness, two-factor authentication
- **Cloud Storage** – Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox workflows

## Module 3 – AI Foundations
Understand how AI works before using it professionally.

- What is Artificial Intelligence? (History, types, and scope)
- Generative AI explained: Text, Image, Audio, and Video
- Large Language Models (LLMs) — how they work and their limitations
- AI Ethics: Bias, hallucinations, responsible AI usage
- Prompt Engineering Basics: The art of talking to AI
