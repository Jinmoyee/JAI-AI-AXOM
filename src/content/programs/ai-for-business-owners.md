## Program Overview
The **AI for Business Owners** program is designed for entrepreneurs who want real results: more leads, less manual work, faster customer responses, and a professional AI-powered online presence. In 2 months, you will learn to implement AI systems that save time, reduce costs, and generate revenue — without needing any technical background.

---

## Week 1–2: AI for Marketing & Social Media
- **AI Content Strategy** — Use ChatGPT to create a 30-day content calendar in minutes
- **Social Media Captions & Hashtags** — AI-generated posts for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn
- **Image Generation for Business** — Canva AI and DALL-E for product images, banners, and ads
- **AI Video Scripts** — Write engaging Reels and YouTube video scripts with ChatGPT
- **Email Marketing with AI** — Subject lines, body copy, and A/B test variations
- **SEO-Optimized Blog Writing** — AI-assisted blog posts that rank on Google

---

## Week 3–4: AI Customer Support & Chatbots
- **What is a Chatbot?** — Types, platforms, and business use cases
- **Chatbase** — Build a custom chatbot trained on your website, FAQs, and product catalog
- **Manychat** — Instagram and Facebook DM automation for leads and support
- **WhatsApp Business Automation** — Set up auto-replies, sequences, and order confirmations
- **AI Customer Support Flow** — Escalation logic, lead qualification, and CRM integration

---

## Week 5–6: AI Website & Lead Generation
- **Lovable / Bolt** — Build a professional AI-generated website with no coding
- **Landing Page Design** — High-converting lead capture pages using AI
- **AI Lead Magnets** — Create free guides, quizzes, and tools to attract leads
- **Lead Generation Automation** — Connect your form to WhatsApp, email, and CRM automatically
- **Google My Business + AI** — Optimize your local listing and auto-generate review responses

---

## Week 7–8: Full Business Automation
- **Make.com** — Map your complete business workflow and automate it visually
- **Zapier** — Connect your booking form, CRM, email, and WhatsApp in one automation
- **AI Business Intelligence** — Use ChatGPT to analyze your sales data and suggest improvements
- **Bonus 1-on-1 Session** — Personalized AI implementation roadmap for your specific business

---

## Business Projects (Deliverables)
1. **AI Content Calendar** — 30 days of social content planned and drafted using ChatGPT
2. **Business Chatbot** — A live chatbot deployed on your website or WhatsApp via Chatbase
3. **AI Landing Page** — A professional lead generation page built with Lovable or Bolt
4. **Automation Workflow** — A Make.com or Zapier pipeline connecting at least 3 business tools
